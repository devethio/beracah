A trading partner built on trust

Beracah Impex FZCO is an international sourcing, wholesale trading and supply chain company in Dubai Silicon Oasis, connecting certified manufacturers with businesses across East Africa and the Middle East.

Who we are

We work between the world's manufacturing markets and the fast-growing economies of East Africa and the Middle East. Our work covers the whole trade cycle: buying bulk raw materials and supplies from certified manufacturers in India, China, Indonesia and the UAE, managing containerised freight and port transit through corridors such as Djibouti, and handling the commercial side of every deal, from proforma structuring to bank settlement and compliance.

Our customers are manufacturers, contractors, fleet operators, distributors and institutions. They need dependable supply, clear specifications and a partner who understands how goods really move into the region.
