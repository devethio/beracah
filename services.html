Sourcing, shipping and settlement under one roof

Three services that cover the full journey of your goods, from the factory floor to your warehouse.

International sourcing and procurement

We procure bulk industrial raw materials, food manufacturing inputs, construction supplies and heavy-duty automotive components directly from certified manufacturers in India, China, Indonesia and the UAE.

  • Supplier selection and vetting
  • Specification and sample approval
  • Price negotiation with manufacturers
  • Pre-shipment quality checks
  • Consolidation of mixed orders
  • Product documents such as COA, SDS and test reports

Supply chain and logistics management

We manage containerised freight end to end: maritime shipping routes, port transit corridors including Djibouti, and inland logistics coordination.

  • Container booking and freight
  • Full and mixed container loads
  • Marine insurance arrangements
  • Shipment tracking and updates
  • Djibouti port transit coordination
  • Inland transport coordination

Trade settlement and compliance

We make cross-border transactions straightforward, from structuring the proforma to bank settlement and regulatory compliance for regional industrial and commercial partners.

  • Proforma invoice structuring
  • LC, CAD and T/T settlement
  • Electronic single window documents
  • Customs clearance support
  • Import permit guidance
  • Institutional tender support

How your order reaches you

One team handles the whole journey, from your first enquiry to delivery at your warehouse.

  1. 01

    Enquiry and sourcing

    Tell us the product, specification and quantity. We match it to a certified manufacturer and confirm specifications or samples.

  2. 02

    Proforma and payment

    We issue a proforma invoice and agree settlement by LC, CAD or T/T, on the delivery terms that suit you.

  3. 03

    Shipping and documents

    We book containerised freight and prepare the bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin and invoices.

  4. 04

    Clearance and delivery

    We coordinate port transit through Djibouti, customs and single window documents, and the inland leg to you.

Documents we prepare

Complete, consistent paperwork is what gets goods through ports and banks on time. We prepare and check every document before it is presented.

Proforma invoicePrice, terms and specifications agreed before you open an LC or pay a deposit.
Commercial invoiceThe final invoice that matches the goods shipped and your payment terms.
Packing listItem-by-item contents, weights and packing for each container.
Bill of ladingThe shipping line's receipt and title document for your cargo.
Certificate of originProof of where goods were made, for customs and duty purposes.
Insurance certificateMarine cover for CIF shipments or when you request it.
Certificate of analysisTest results for food ingredients and chemicals, batch by batch.
Single window documentsElectronic import declarations prepared for your clearing agent.
