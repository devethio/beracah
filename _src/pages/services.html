Documents we prepare

Complete, consistent paperwork is what gets goods through ports and banks on time. We prepare and check every document before it is presented.

Proforma invoicePrice, terms and specifications agreed before you open an LC or pay a deposit.
Commercial invoiceThe final invoice that matches the goods shipped and your payment terms.
Packing listItem-by-item contents, weights and packing for each container.
Bill of ladingThe shipping line's receipt and title document for your cargo.
Certificate of originProof of where goods were made, for customs and duty purposes.
Insurance certificateMarine cover for CIF shipments or when you request it.
Certificate of analysisTest results for food ingredients and chemicals, batch by batch.
Single window documentsElectronic import declarations prepared for your clearing agent.
