How your order reaches you

One team handles the whole journey, from your first enquiry to delivery at your warehouse.

{{ship}}
  1. 01

    Enquiry and sourcing

    Tell us the product, specification and quantity. We match it to a certified manufacturer and confirm specifications or samples.

  2. 02

    Proforma and payment

    We issue a proforma invoice and agree settlement by LC, CAD or T/T, on the delivery terms that suit you.

  3. 03

    Shipping and documents

    We book containerised freight and prepare the bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin and invoices.

  4. 04

    Clearance and delivery

    We coordinate port transit through Djibouti, customs and single window documents, and the inland leg to you.
